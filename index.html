About Us

Welcome to Serendib Spice & Grill, where the vibrant spirit of Sri Lanka meets the art of modern grilling. Our passion lies in crafting dishes that blend fire, flavor, and tradition. Each plate is kissed by the flame and seasoned with the essence of island spices — cinnamon, clove, cardamom, and chili. From our signature grills to comforting rice and curry, every meal tells a story of warmth, heritage, and home. Whether you dine in or take away, we invite you to experience the true soul of Serendib — one bite at a time.

Our passionate chefs combine traditional techniques with modern innovation to create unforgettable dining experiences. Whether you're joining us for a romantic dinner, family celebration, or business meeting, we promise exceptional service and extraordinary flavors.


At Serendib, every meal is a celebration, and every guest is family.
